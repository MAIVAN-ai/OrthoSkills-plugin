# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of professional background, level of clinical experience, age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing clinical viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for patients and for the open-source orthopaedic AI community
- Showing empathy toward other community members, especially trainees and junior contributors
- Citing sources, acknowledging uncertainty, and being explicit about the limits of evidence

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- **Sharing identifiable patient data without consent** — this is a hard line and may also be a legal violation in your jurisdiction
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional clinical or open-source setting

## Patient data

This is an orthopaedic project. Patient confidentiality is non-negotiable. Any contribution containing identifiable patient information — names, dates of birth, medical record numbers, identifying photos, identifying radiographs (e.g. visible jewellery, surgical markings unique to a patient), institutional identifiers without consent — will be removed immediately and the contributor's commit history rewritten if necessary.

Always anonymise. When in doubt, do not share.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the maintainers by opening a confidential issue or contacting them directly via GitHub. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
